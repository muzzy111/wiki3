# Research Brief Protocol

> How the Researcher role accepts briefs and writes completions.

## Acceptance

A brief arrives at `3.0RESEARCHER/CHAT/Inbox/<...>.md`. The Researcher confirms the question is scoped and reads related prior research in the Karpathy wiki.

## Output

Per `3.0RESEARCHER/RULES/Research Protocol.md`:
- Sections: Question, Scope, Sources, Findings, Caveats, Suggested Follow-ups.
- Inline `[N]` citations.
