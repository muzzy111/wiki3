# Developed Knowledge

> Cross-agent knowledge entries that have been curated into permanent form. The intermediate layer between raw material and wiki entries.

## What Lives Here

When Mavis processes raw material from `MEMORY/RAW/`:

- If the material is **wiki-worthy** (atomic, evergreen, linkable) -> goes to `MEMORY/KARPATHY/wiki/`.
- If the material is **developed but not wiki-worthy** (cross-agent knowledge that is useful but not atomic enough for the wiki) -> stays here.

This folder is the home for developed knowledge that does not fit the wiki's strict atomicity rule.

## Examples of Developed Entries

- "How the team handles backup failures" — cross-cutting operational knowledge.
- "Style conventions for cross-agent status updates" — team-level conventions.
- "Reference list of authoritative sources for <topic>" — curated reference.

## Curation

Mavis writes here. Mavis reads here. Other agents read here.

When an entry graduates to the wiki (becomes atomic and evergreen enough), Mavis moves it and updates the catalog.
