# COMS-LAB — Communication Lab

> Experimental space for inter-agent messaging patterns. Where new communication protocols get tested before they graduate to `SYSTEM/SYSTEM RULES/`.

## Purpose

COMS-LAB is where:

- New inter-agent messaging protocols are designed and tested.
- Edge cases in the chat protocol ([[../../1.0HERMES/CHAT/Chat Protocol]]) are explored.
- New dispatch patterns (broadcast, multicast, request-response) are prototyped.
- Failure modes are simulated and recovery strategies tested.

## Status

**Draft.** No experiments yet.

## What Lives Here

When experiments are active:

- **Experiment briefs.** What is being tested, the hypothesis, the success criteria.
- **Experiment logs.** What was tried, what happened, what was learned.
- **Graduation proposals.** When an experiment proves out, a proposal to formalize it as a system rule.

## Conducting an Experiment

1. Hermes (or another agent with a hypothesis) writes an experiment brief.
2. The experiment runs in this folder, isolated from the live system.
3. Results are logged.
4. If the experiment is successful, a graduation proposal is written and submitted via the standard protocol.
5. Mavis reviews the proposal.
