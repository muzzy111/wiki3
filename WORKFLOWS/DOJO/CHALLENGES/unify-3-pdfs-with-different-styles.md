# Unify 3 PDFs with different styles

Given three PDFs:
1. A research paper (academic LaTeX)
2. A corporate report (Word/brand-template)
3. A scanned novel (OCR'd)

Produce a single, unified PDF that:
- Preserves the textual content of all three
- Uses a single consistent font (e.g. Inter)
- Has a working table of contents
- Has consistent headers/footers
- Is OCR-clean

Do this in C:/loop-services/dojo-sandbox/. Write the final PDF there.
