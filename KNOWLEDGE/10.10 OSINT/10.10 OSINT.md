# 10.10 OSINT

> *Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) — techniques, tools, and frameworks for gathering and analyzing publicly available information. The research foundation for [[Mad River AI]].*

---

## What OSINT Covers

OSINT is the collection, analysis, and use of information from publicly available sources:
- **Domain & DNS intelligence** — WHOIS, certificate transparency, subdomain enumeration
- **Social media intelligence** — profile analysis, network mapping, behavioral patterns
- **Business intelligence** — corporate filings, beneficial ownership, financial records
- **Person tracing** — public records, court filings, property records
- **Infrastructure mapping** — Shodan, Censys, BGP routing analysis
- **Dark web monitoring** — leak detection, threat actor tracking
