# Third-party integrations for JIT access

Last validated Nov 12, 2025

Use third-party integrations to provide just-in-time (JIT) access to your Tailscale network (known as a tailnet). Use these integrations to add, update, or remove group and user settings in your [tailnet policy file][kb-tailnet-policy-file].
