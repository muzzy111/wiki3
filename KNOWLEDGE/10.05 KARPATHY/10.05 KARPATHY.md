## The 3-Folder Wiki System

Karpathy's methodology organizes knowledge into three buckets:
1. **RAW** — unprocessed captures, immediate notes, links, clippings
2. **PROCESSED** — synthesized, linked, refined notes
3. **ARCHIVE** — completed, stable knowledge

This system is the architectural DNA for how this vault handles knowledge flow.
