# Jobs Log

> Aggregated summaries of work completed across the team. Hermes maintains.

## What Lives Here

- **Weekly digest.** What tasks were completed this week, by whom, with what outcome.
- **Monthly digest.** Higher-level summary of trends, blockers, and accomplishments.
- **Custom aggregates.** Ad-hoc summaries Mavis or Hermes request.

## Cadence

- **Weekly digest.** Every Sunday, after Mavis' weekly audit.
- **Monthly digest.** First day of each month.
- **Custom.** On request.

## File Naming

`<YYYY-Www> — Weekly Digest.md` (ISO week) or `<YYYY-MM> — Monthly Digest.md`.

## Schema

Each digest includes:

- Total tasks completed.
- Tasks by agent.
- Tasks by status (done, failed, cancelled).
- Notable deliverables.
- Open blockers.
- Audit findings (from Mavis' weekly audit).
